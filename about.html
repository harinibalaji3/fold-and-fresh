About Fold & Fresh

We are a laundry service built by people who got tired of ruined sweaters and lost turnaround times.

Company overview

Fold & Fresh picks up, cleans and delivers laundry and dry cleaning across the city.

We started after one too many garments came back from a corner-store cleaner shrunk, faded or simply late. Now we run our own facility, train our own team, and check every order before it goes back out the door.

Our name is simple: fold the laundry, keep it fresh.

Our mission

Give people their time back by making laundry and dry cleaning effortless, reliable and honestly priced.

Our vision

A city where nobody’s evening or weekend is spent standing over a washing machine.

Our journey

From a single route van to a full-scale commercial fleet.

2019

The First Route

Fold & Fresh launches with one van and a rented facility corner.

2021

Flagship Facility

Our own automated cleaning facility opens with in-house quality controls.

2023

Live Tracking

Order tracking launches, letting customers follow garment stages live.

2025

B2B Expansion

Commercial linen plans launch for boutique hotels, gyms, and Airbnb hosts.

2026

City Coverage

Now covering eight city zones with express same-day turnaround.
